DAC - Discretionary Access Control (DAC) is an authorization model where the owner of the resource decides who is allowed to access it
MAC - Mandatory Access Control (MAC) is an authorization model where access to resources is determined by a set of rules defined by a central authority
ABAC - Attribute Based Access Control (ABAC) determines access through a combination of contexts and system wide attributes
Data Controller - An individual or entity that determines the purposes and means of processing personal data. They have primary responsibility for ensuring the data's protection and compliance with privacy regulations (Determines purpose)
Data Processor - The Data Processor processes data on behalf of the Data Controller, following the controller’s instructions (Preforms Procssing)
Data Custodian - typically responsible for ensuring the safety and maintenance of data assets through its various stages of storage, but doesn't decide on processing methods (Manages infrastructure)
